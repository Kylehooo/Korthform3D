# Korthform3D

A simple 3D modeler for 3D printing that runs in your browser. Drag shapes onto the mat, turn some into holes, group them, and export an STL for your slicer.

It's one file. Download `korthform3d-v12.html`, open it in your browser, and start building. No install, no account.

## What it does

- Basic shapes and holes, with group and ungroup
- Dimensions and constraints between shapes
- Dot shape tool for custom outlines
- Screw maker for metric screws, nuts and threaded holes
- STL import and export
- Twelve mat themes

Units are millimetres and Z is up, the same as your slicer. Press `?` in the app for shortcuts.

Your designs are saved in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
